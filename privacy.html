Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how torinado ("we", "us"), an indie game team, handles data in our internal tools that connect to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube ("the Service").

1. Data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the Service reads data of torinado's own accounts only:

  • Basic profile information (account ID, username, display name)
  • Account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts)
  • Our own public videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, post date)
  • Comments on our own videos (to know when viewers write to us)
  • On YouTube, the Service also uploads our own videos and sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails

We do not access data of other users.

2. How we use it

We use this data only to analyze how our own videos perform and to plan future content. We do not sell, share or use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored on a private computer and a private repository managed by our team. Tokens are kept in a secrets manager or in files readable only by the Service.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep statistics as long as they are useful for our analysis. You can revoke the Service's access at any time from your TikTok or Instagram settings, or for YouTube from the Google Account security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). On request we delete stored data within 30 days. To request deletion of data, contact us.

5. YouTube API Services

The Service uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page.
